How to play

W A S D / ArrowsMove the hero you control. Tap or click open ground to walk there.
Click an enemyTarget it and start auto-attacking. Tab cycles nearby enemies.
1 – 6Abilities. Hover a slot to read what it does. New ones unlock as you level.
QSwap between Orhun and Handan. The other is played by the AI and fights with you.
FLanternburst when the jade gauge is full: heals both heroes and scorches nearby enemies.
RDrink a Healing Draught (shared 20s cooldown).
ShiftDodge in the direction you are moving. Orhun rolls, Handan steps through the jade. Attacks pass through you while dodging. In co-op: Left Shift for Orhun, Right Shift for Handan.
T · V · ZT opens waystone travel (relighting a beacon wakes that zone's waystone; Lanternhold's is always open). V: Handan conjures Lumen to carry you both, much faster, outside of fights. Z: auto-cast on/off, abilities fire by themselves during fights.
ControllerLeft stick moves · A B X Y and bumpers cast abilities 1–6 · RT dodge · LT draught · D-pad up talk · D-pad down Lanternburst · D-pad left/right target · Back swaps hero. Two controllers play together in co-op.
E / SpaceTalk, pick up glowing quest items, relight beacons, advance dialogue.
C · L (or X) · H · EscBags and gear · Quest log · This help · Close windows / clear target.
RolesOrhun is the duelist off-tank: he holds threat with Sunder and Challenging Roar and hits hard with Executioner's Edge. Handan is the loreweaver: damage and roots from range, plus Mending Light and Jade Ward to keep Orhun standing. Lumen, her spirit fox, fights at her side.
